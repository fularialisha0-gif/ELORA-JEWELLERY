ÉLORA Cinematic Video Assets Directory
=====================================

Place optional high-bitrate MP4 campaign loops in this folder:
- /public/videos/hero-jewellery.mp4
- /public/videos/diamond-closeup.mp4
- /public/videos/jewellery-editorial.mp4

When local MP4 streams are not supplied, CinematicVideoFrame automatically
renders a hardware-accelerated motion plate backed by the Maison's 16:9
studio campaign photography with interactive Play/Pause and ambient sound controls.
